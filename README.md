<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">GPS tracking allows dispatch teams to monitor drivers and helps customers understand where their orders are. Route optimization can organize delivery stops according to location, traffic conditions, and scheduled time windows.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Customers may miss deliveries simply because they do not know when a driver will arrive. Automated SMS, push notifications, and in-app alerts can communicate order confirmation, dispatch status, estimated arrival times, and delivery updates.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Incorrect addresses can waste time and create unnecessary delivery attempts. Address autocomplete, map pins, GPS-based location sharing, and additional delivery instructions help customers provide clearer information.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Before dispatch, the application can flag missing address details for review. This allows the operations team to resolve address-related problems before the driver begins the journey.</span></p>
<h3><strong>4. Digital Proof of Delivery</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Digital proof of delivery creates a record of completed orders using timestamps, electronic signatures, one-time passwords, or photographs where appropriate.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">When a delivery fails, drivers can record the reason, such as an unavailable recipient or an incorrect address. Administrators can review these records, contact customers, and arrange another attempt when necessary.</span></p>
